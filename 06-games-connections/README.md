# 06 · Games connections

The games this repo grew under don't teach with lecterns — they whisper, and occasionally all-out sing, about maths, electronics, and physics while being games. This branch is the songbook: for each game, a map from its moments to the *rootstock* pages (and bibliography links) holding the foundations of those moments.

It serves two readers. A **player** who felt a spark mid-game — *what was that equation on the victory screen actually saying?* — can land here and walk to the real thing, free and unhurried. And the **builder** (hello, Clemence) can treat each map as a content checklist: every game moment pointing at a not-yet-written page is a quiet to-do.

## House understanding

Game text and repo text share a covenant, borrowed from *FloralFight*'s own design language: setbacks are **understanding delayed, never denied**. A player who loses a match still keeps the equation; a reader who bounces off a page still keeps the page. Nothing locks, in either world.
