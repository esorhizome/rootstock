# FloralFight — the foundations map

*FloralFight (a worthy opponent)* is the fighter aspect of *An Isolate Grows Roots*: FLORA — a bioluminescent flower with fiber-optic LEDs sewn along her stem — spars with the forest's old, play-hungry residents. Equation fragments resonate out of the opponent's core at every landed hit; win, and the equation decodes into plain words; lose, and the fragments stay yours, meaning withheld for now — *understanding delayed, never denied*. A K.O. is a **SWEPT!** — an involuntary nap, never an ending.

This page maps the game's moments to the foundations underneath them.

## The four disciplines and their equations

Each fighting discipline resonates a different equation out of the boss's core. Each row below is a walkable path from game moment to real understanding:

| Discipline | Equation | Plain-words twin | Foundations in this repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Melee (spiky-petaled brawler) | `V = I × R` | push = flow × squeeze | [Push, flow, squeeze](../03-electronics/01-push-flow-squeeze.md) → [First circuit on paper](../03-electronics/02-first-circuit-on-paper.md) → [Electricity meets fabric](../04-physics-for-e-fashion/01-electricity-meets-fabric.md) |
| Magic (layered-lotus magician) | `P = I × V` | spending = flow × push | [Power, and where it goes](../03-electronics/03-power-and-where-it-goes.md) → [Battery budgets](../04-physics-for-e-fashion/03-battery-budgets.md) |
| Archer (swept-petal, thorn-bow) | `F = m·a` | force = mass × how-fast-speed-changes | [The most important line](../02-programming-maths/02-the-most-important-line.md) (gravity's two lines *are* the arrow's arc) → [Seams, strain, and motion](../04-physics-for-e-fashion/04-seams-strain-and-motion.md) (the same law, moonlighting in costume design) |
| Mine-layer (tulip-cup; seed-pod mines) | `E = ½CV²` | stored splash = half × cup-size × push² | Capacitor page: on [branch 03's map](../03-electronics/README.md), growing. Meanwhile the twin above holds, plus one luminous detail below. |

**The mine-layer's luminous detail:** that little ² means stored energy grows with the *square* of the push — double the volts, **four times** the splash. A capacitor is a component that stores energy and releases it all at once, which is why the prototype pairs it with mines: the mechanic *is* the physics. (The ½ is honest bookkeeping from how the cup fills — gently at first, harder as it fills; its full story belongs to the future capacitor page.)

## Moments and their machinery

**Petals as the health meter.** FLORA's corolla thins petal by petal as hits land, and regrows petal by petal in victory — each petal's regrowth is a [lerp](../02-programming-maths/03-lerp-the-quiet-workhorse.md) from scale 0 to 1, staggered for grace. The character *is* the number; the number is animated by branch 02.

**Fragments on every second landed hit.** A fixed schedule wearing randomness's clothes — the kindest drop design for a learning game, named and honoured in [Randomness that feels fair](../02-programming-maths/04-randomness-that-feels-fair.md).

**The victory sim's warning.** Push 9 volts through a small squeeze and the sewn-in LED would cook: that warning is `P = I × V` performing live — [Power](../03-electronics/03-power-and-where-it-goes.md) has the full account, and [Safety with small volts](../03-electronics/04-safety-with-small-volts.md) the standing habits.

**Arena and arrows.** FLORA's position is a [coordinate pair](../01-maths/03-the-grid-under-everything.md); every motion on screen is [the most important line](../02-programming-maths/02-the-most-important-line.md) at sixty heartbeats a second.

**The in-game Bibliography / Lookbook.** The game's plan calls for a main-menu bibliography of maker research, checked regularly to stay retrievable. That is [07-bibliography](../07-bibliography/README.md), which doubles as its staging ground — same vetting flags, same re-check ritual. "MAKE IT REAL" links route naturally: Ohm's law → [SparkFun's LilyPad tutorials](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/tags/lilypad?page=all); the power family → [Adafruit's wearables guides](https://learn.adafruit.com/category/wearables); details per equation in [e-fashion](../07-bibliography/e-fashion.md).
