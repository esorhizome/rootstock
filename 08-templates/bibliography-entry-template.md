# Bibliography entry template

Every entry in [07-bibliography/](../07-bibliography/) uses this shape. Copy, fill, date.

```markdown
### <Resource name>
<https://example.org/>
**What:** <One line: what it actually is.>
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely | ◐ freemium — <state exactly what is free and what is paid>
**Account:** none | optional (<for what>) | ⚠ required
**Fit:** <One line: format (interactive / video / text), chunk size, and anything that matters
for readers with ADHD, memory difficulties, or dysgraphia.>
**Caveat:** <ads, age of content, difficulty level — or omit the line>
*Last checked: YYYY-MM-DD*
```

## Flags

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ | Free entirely. |
| ◐ | Freemium — the free part is stated precisely, never vaguely. |
| ⚠ | Account required to use meaningfully. Allowed in the bibliography, but always flagged. |
| ⌛ | Archived or unmaintained, still working and worth reading. |
| ✗ | Removed — kept as a tombstone with the reason and date, never silently deleted. |

## Standards for inclusion

Free or honestly-flagged freemium. Legally hosted (official sites, open-licensed copies — nothing pirated). Account-optional strongly preferred. Gentle formats earn priority: interactive things to click and drag, short video chunks, plain-language text — anything that teaches without demanding handwriting or long unbroken attention.

The re-checking ritual lives in [07-bibliography/README.md](../07-bibliography/README.md).
