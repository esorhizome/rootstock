# rootstock

*The ground layer under the games: maths, the maths inside code, electronics, and the physics of clothing that glows.*

A **rootstock** is the hardy base plant that a graft grows out of. This repo is that base. Games like *An Isolate Grows Roots* (and its fighter minigame, *FloralFight*) whisper — sometimes all-out sing — about maths, electronics engineering, and physics. Everything they whisper about traces back to material collected here, where anyone can read it slowly, for free, with no account and no timer.

Begun by Clemence, August 2026. Meant to exist indefinitely.

## Who this is for

People with ADHD. People whose memory drops things — often, and through no fault of theirs. People with dysgraphia, for whom handwriting is a genuine obstacle rather than a study tool. People who tend to talk themselves down, and who also flinch at over-sweetened praise. People who quietly assumed engineering, physics, and maths belong to others — some distant, gifted crowd.

That last assumption is wrong, and this repo is built as a long, patient counter-argument. The full design of *how* it teaches — the working agreements every page follows — lives in [HOW-THIS-TEACHES.md](HOW-THIS-TEACHES.md).

## Three ways to use this repo

1. **Read and research, standalone.** Every branch below works on its own. Enter anywhere. Leave anywhere. Nothing tracks you.
2. **Follow the bibliography.** [07-bibliography/](07-bibliography/) is a vetted, dated collection of learning links that are free, legal, and (wherever possible) usable without creating an account. Each entry says exactly what is free and what is not.
3. **Trace the games.** [06-games-connections/](06-games-connections/) maps game moments — an equation resonating out of a boss's core, petals regrowing after a match — to the pages here that hold their foundations.

## Standing promises

This repo itself never requires an account, never keeps score, and never runs a clock. Links in the bibliography are free and legally hosted, with account requirements stated plainly — an entry that needs sign-up is flagged, never smuggled in. Formulas always travel with a plain-words twin. Nothing here requires handwriting. And a reader who disappears for eight months returns to a repo that asks for no apology and no catch-up.
