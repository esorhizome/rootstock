# Prompt used to generate this repo

## I really want to have a comprehensive way to learn maths in the long term. 

### Target audience and teaching style

- people with ADHD and/or moderate/near-severe memory-difficulties
- people who are self-discouragement-prone, but also excessive-praise-discomforted
   - encourage lightly + often, 
   - but: do not ever venture into sycophancy
- people without a natural gift for maths whatsoever
- people with dysgraphia 
- people who assume that engineering + physics + maths are for others far away up on a pedestal
- people who are interested in electronic fashion


### Make this a public repo...

- about maths
- about programming maths
- about electronics engineering with regard to maths + physics
- about physics with regard to DIY electronic fashion

### Sustainability: make sure viewers can...
- read/research standalone
    - find a bibliography with up-to-date links
        - majority free or freemium (always legal, nothing pirated)
        - majority account-creation optional, not mandatory
- learn more about where my games' foundations come from.
