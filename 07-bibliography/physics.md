# Bibliography · Physics

Flags: ✓ free · ◐ freemium · ⚠ account required · ⌛ archived · ✗ resting. Legend and ritual in [README.md](README.md).

## Play first

### PhET Interactive Simulations
<https://phet.colorado.edu/>
**What:** The university-built simulation library: forces, motion, energy, waves, electricity, light — all draggable.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (not needed for sims)
**Fit:** The single best "feel the physics before reading the physics" resource; no typing, no writing, no account.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Courses and gentle lessons

### Khan Academy — Physics
<https://www.khanacademy.org/science/physics>
**What:** Short videos + practice across mechanics, energy, waves, electricity.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (progress only)
**Fit:** Bite-sized, hint-friendly, penalty-free.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### The Physics Classroom ◐
<https://www.physicsclassroom.com/>
**What:** Patient tutorials at a comfortable reading level, paired with free HTML5 interactives ("Concept Builders").
**Cost:** ◐ tutorials + interactives free; small paid extras are teacher-aimed (an optional ~$3/yr removes ads)
**Account:** optional (ad-removal only)
**Fit:** Short concept pages plus genuinely clickable practice — a strong middle shelf between PhET's play and the textbooks below.
**Caveat:** Ads on the free tier; physics only, no electronics.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Reference shelves

### HyperPhysics
<https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html>
**What:** Georgia State's venerable concept-map encyclopedia — every physics idea a clickable node linked to its neighbours.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** For looking one thing up and seeing what it touches; not a course, no hand-holding.
**Caveat:** Note the address — the site moved from its old `phy-astr.gsu.edu` home, whose certificate now fails; use the URL above.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Physics LibreTexts
<https://phys.libretexts.org/>
**What:** Open-access library of physics textbooks and course maps.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none to read
**Fit:** A reference forest; visit with a specific question in hand.
**Caveat:** Dense academic formatting, varies by book.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### OpenStax — Physics volumes
<https://openstax.org/subjects/science>
**What:** Peer-reviewed openly-licensed full textbooks: *College Physics*, *University Physics* I–III, and siblings.
**Cost:** ✓ free online + free PDF (print costs)
**Account:** none to read
**Fit:** Rigorous backbone for someday; not chunked for ADHD reading, honestly noted.
**Caveat:** The subjects index page needs JavaScript; individual book pages load plainly.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*
