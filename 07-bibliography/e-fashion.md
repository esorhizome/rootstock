# Bibliography · E-fashion and wearables

Flags: ✓ free · ◐ freemium · ⚠ account required · ⌛ archived · ✗ resting. Legend and ritual in [README.md](README.md).

The shelf closest to FLORA's petals. These are the sources the games' "MAKE IT REAL" links will draw from (see [the FloralFight map](../06-games-connections/floral-fight.md)).

## Core tutorial libraries

### Adafruit Learning System — Wearables
<https://learn.adafruit.com/category/wearables>
**What:** 300+ free guides for LED clothing, costumes, and accessories — the largest wearables tutorial collection anywhere.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely (Adafruit sells parts; the learning never costs)
**Account:** optional
**Fit:** Photo-rich step-by-step projects across all levels; where "battery-sizing for the power family" guides live.
**Caveat:** Projects assume Adafruit-brand parts; the physics transfers to any brand.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### SparkFun — LilyPad tutorials
<https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/tags/lilypad?page=all>
**What:** ~60 tutorials on sewable electronics: e-sewing basics, component guides, full garment projects.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** Crafts-first framing (needle before breadboard) — the natural landing page for [Ohm's-law-in-fabric](../04-physics-for-e-fashion/01-electricity-meets-fabric.md).
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### FashionLacrum — Beginner's guide to e-textiles
<https://www.fashionlacrum.com/articles/beginner-e-textilessoft-circuits>
**What:** A single, well-made beginner guide: materials, components, three worked circuits, sewing tips — from a fashion-side voice.
**Cost:** ✓ free (downloadable PDF included)
**Account:** none
**Fit:** Short and focused; the PDF makes a fine offline companion.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Chibitronics — free lesson plans
<https://chibitronics.com/education/lesson-plans/>
**What:** Free lessons for peel-and-stick "circuit sticker" paper circuits — electronics meets papercraft and storytelling.
**Cost:** ✓ lesson content free (the physical stickers are a product)
**Account:** none
**Fit:** The gentlest possible on-ramp: stickers before needles, paper before fabric — a lovely first project for shaky-confidence days.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Project galleries and voices

### Instructables — Wearables ◐
<https://www.instructables.com/circuits/wearables/>
**What:** Crowd-submitted wearable projects, photographed step by step.
**Cost:** ◐ reading free; PDFs/ad-free need membership
**Account:** optional (saving/commenting only)
**Fit:** Huge variety; quality varies with the crowd — read comments before committing an afternoon.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### WearableTutorials.com
<https://www.wearabletutorials.com/>
**What:** An independent maker's blog on LED fashion, soft circuits, and fiber optics for cosplay and costume.
**Cost:** ✓ articles free (the site also [sells kits](https://www.wearabletutorials.com/diy-wearable-technology-kit-shop/) — that page is a shop, not a lesson)
**Account:** none
**Fit:** Costume-forward inspiration with practical builds.
**Caveat:** Small site; check post dates.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Becky Stern
<https://beckystern.com/>
**What:** Portfolio and tutorials from a longtime wearables teacher (formerly Adafruit's wearables lead); her video back-catalog is a masterclass shelf.
**Cost:** ◐ site and videos free; optional supporter tiers
**Account:** none
**Fit:** A trusted personal voice — good for "how does a professional actually think through a build."
**Caveat:** Current work spans general making, not only e-textiles.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Hackster — screen-printed wearable circuits
<https://www.hackster.io/suryudey/diy-wearable-electronics-8c1340>
**What:** A tutorial for screen-printing conductive-ink circuits directly onto stretch fabric.
**Cost:** ✓ free to read
**Account:** none to read
**Fit:** Author-rated *advanced*, ~6 hours, specialty supplies — a horizon project, filed for the future on purpose.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### MIT High-Low Tech ⌛
<https://highlowtech.org/>
**What:** The archived research group (2009–2014) whose work seeded much of today's e-textile world.
**Cost:** ✓ free
**Account:** none
**Fit:** ⌛ Historical depth and ideas; tools and links show their age. Read for lineage, not shopping lists.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

<https://www.adafruit.com/>

**Cost:** shop (the learning half is the free entry above)
**Account:** optional (checkout only)
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Resting ✗

### Kobakant — "How To Get What You Want" ✗
`kobakant.at/DIY/`
**What it was:** The most comprehensive DIY e-textile technique reference on the web — swatches, sensors, stitches — by Hannah Perner-Wilson and Mika Satomi. Beloved, and deservedly.
**Why it rests:** Found serving injected spam (a compromised site) on 2026-08-19, verified across multiple pages and fetches. Do not browse it until repaired.
**Hope:** Sites get cleaned. Re-check on ritual days; if it returns healthy, it goes straight back to the core shelf. Snapshots of the healthy site exist in the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine for the patient.
*Rested: 2026-08-19*
