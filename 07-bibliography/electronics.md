## Simulators — build circuits with zero components

### Falstad Circuit Simulator
<https://www.falstad.com/circuit/>
**What:** The long-loved in-browser simulator: build circuits, watch animated current actually flow.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** Seeing the moving dots slow down as resistance rises teaches Ohm's law faster than any paragraph; huge example library.
**Caveat:** Interface is dated and dense at first — arrive via the examples menu.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### FreeCircuitSim
<https://freecircuitsim.com/learn/>
**What:** Browser simulator (built on the open-source CircuitJS1) wrapped in short leveled lessons — beginner, intermediate, advanced.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** The lesson wrapper gives Falstad-style simulation a gentler front door.
**Caveat:** Small independent site — shorter track record than the institutions.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### PhET — Circuit Construction Kit
<https://phet.colorado.edu/>
**What:** University of Colorado's playful drag-and-drop circuit sims (and much more science besides).
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (not needed for sims)
**Fit:** The friendliest possible first circuit-touching experience.
**Caveat:** Educational-simplified, not an engineering tool — which is the point.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Wokwi ◐
<https://wokwi.com/>
**What:** In-browser microcontroller simulator — write code, wire virtual Arduinos/ESP32s, watch them run.
**Cost:** ◐ core simulator free; paid tier is for pro tooling
**Account:** optional (only to sync saved projects)
**Fit:** For the day sewable microcontrollers arrive; code-centric, a step past LED basics.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### CircuitVerse
<https://circuitverse.org/>
**What:** Open-source browser designer/simulator for digital logic — gates, flip-flops, timing diagrams.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely, open source
**Account:** optional (cloud-save only)
**Fit:** Zero-signup and visual; for the *digital* branch of electronics, after analog fundamentals.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Tinkercad Circuits ⚠
<https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits>
**What:** Autodesk's beginner-oriented drag-and-drop circuit sim with virtual Arduinos.
**Cost:** ✓ free
**Account:** ⚠ required — confirmed: no building or simulating without an Autodesk sign-in
**Fit:** Lovely tool behind a mandatory gate; listed for completeness, outranked here by Falstad/PhET which demand nothing.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Courses and tutorials

### CircuitBread
<https://www.circuitbread.com/tutorials>
**What:** Electronics tutorial hub — 250+ mostly-video tutorials plus reference tools and open textbooks; "always free."
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (community features)
**Fit:** Short video chunks; find the "Circuits 101" series for the true-beginner track.
**Caveat:** The tutorials index surfaces intermediate content first — search for the beginner series by name.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Khan Academy — Electrical Engineering
<https://www.khanacademy.org/science/electrical-engineering>
**What:** Khan's circuits/EE sequence: short videos, articles, practice.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (progress only)
**Fit:** Bite-sized and shame-free, per house standards.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### SparkFun Learn
<https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials>
**What:** 950+ hookup guides and concept tutorials from the maker retailer — including the e-textile LilyPad line.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional
**Fit:** Photo-heavy step-by-step guides.
**Caveat:** Guides assume SparkFun parts; concepts transfer regardless.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Adafruit Learning System
<https://learn.adafruit.com/>
**What:** 3,000+ free step-by-step guides across electronics, LEDs, wearables, CircuitPython.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional
**Fit:** Highly visual, project-first; the wearables category gets its own entry in [e-fashion.md](e-fashion.md).
**Caveat:** Assumes Adafruit-brand parts often; filter by "beginner."
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Electrical4U
<https://www.electrical4u.com/>
**What:** Plain-English electrical engineering articles plus thousands of practice questions.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (newsletter only)
**Fit:** Reference articles; visually busy with ads — read in reader mode.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Electronics Tutorials ◐
<https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/>
**What:** Long-running topic-organized basics site, 30+ categories.
**Cost:** ◐ all core tutorials free; optional paid ebook downloads
**Account:** optional (ebooks only)
**Fit:** Text reference; ads and sign-up prompts are the tax.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

## Open textbooks — the deep shelf

### Lessons in Electric Circuits (All About Circuits edition)
<https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/>
**What:** The actively maintained modern edition of Kuphaldt's classic open six-volume circuits textbook.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** The full staircase, DC through semiconductors, when a topic deserves depth.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Lessons in Electric Circuits (author's original) ⌛
<https://www.ibiblio.org/kuphaldt/electricCircuits/>
**What:** Tony Kuphaldt's own canonical open-licensed hosting — HTML, PDF, and source.
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** none
**Fit:** ⌛ A stable archive of the same classic; the All About Circuits edition above is the living copy.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*

### Workforce LibreTexts — Electronics Technology shelf
<https://workforce.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Electronics_Technology>
**What:** Open bookshelf of ~9 electronics texts, including the Kuphaldt volumes (e.g. [the AC volume](https://workforce.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Electronics_Technology/Electric_Circuits_II_-_Alternating_Current_(Kuphaldt))).
**Cost:** ✓ free entirely
**Account:** optional (bookmarking)
**Fit:** Same material, LibreTexts furniture; useful when its chapter navigation suits you better.
*Last checked: 2026-08-19*
